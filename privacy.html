Privacy Policy

Last updated May 3, 2026

Overview

iMara Blast is a desktop tool for publishing social media content. This policy explains how information is handled in connection with your use of the app.

What we do not collect

iMara Blast does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. Specifically:

  • No analytics or tracking tools are used
  • No user data is sent to any server operated by us
  • No cookies or tracking technologies are used
  • No personal information is sold or shared

Authentication tokens

iMara Blast uses OAuth tokens to authenticate with TikTok, Facebook, and Instagram. These tokens are stored exclusively on the user's local machine and are never transmitted to any server we operate. They are sent only to the respective platform APIs as required for publishing.

Content

Media files processed through the app are handled locally and sent directly to the selected social media platforms. No media is stored or retained by iMara Blast.

Third-party platforms

Publishing through iMara Blast involves interaction with third-party platforms governed by their own privacy policies: TikTok, Meta, X (Twitter).
