1. Overview

iMara Blast ("the App") is a desktop tool designed to publish social media content across multiple platforms simultaneously. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information in connection with your use of the App.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

iMara Blast does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data from users or third parties. Specifically:

  • No analytics or tracking tools are used
  • No user data is sent to any third-party server operated by us
  • No cookies or similar tracking technologies are used
  • No personal information is sold, rented, or shared with any party

3. Authentication Tokens

iMara Blast uses OAuth tokens to authenticate with TikTok, Facebook, and Instagram on behalf of the account owner. These tokens are:

  • Stored exclusively on the user's local machine (in a .env file)
  • Never transmitted to any server operated by iMara Blast
  • Only sent directly to the respective platform APIs (TikTok, Meta) as required for publishing

4. Content

Media files (images and videos) uploaded through the App are temporarily processed on the user's local machine and sent directly to the selected social media platforms. No media is stored or retained by iMara Blast.

5. Third-Party Platforms

When you publish content through iMara Blast, you interact with third-party platforms (TikTok, Meta, X/Twitter). Your use of those platforms is governed by their respective privacy policies:

  • TikTok Privacy Policy
  • Meta Privacy Policy
  • X (Twitter) Privacy Policy
